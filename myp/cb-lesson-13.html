Notes: Coordination in plants

tropism

A growth response towards or away from a stimulus. For example: shoots grow towards light.

phototropism

Growth in response to light. For example: a plant on a windowsill.

gravitropism

Growth in response to gravity. For example: roots grow down.

auxin

A plant hormone that controls growth. For example: made in the shoot tip.

positive / negative

Towards / away from the stimulus. For example: shoots: positive phototropism.

ethylene

A plant hormone gas that ripens fruit. For example: bananas in a ripening room.
