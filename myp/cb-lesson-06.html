Notes: Investigating reaction time

reaction time

The time between a stimulus and the response. For example: about 0.2 s.

independent variable

The one thing you change. For example: dominant or other hand.

dependent variable

What you measure. For example: distance the ruler falls.

control variable

Things you keep the same. For example: same ruler, same catcher position.

hypothesis

A prediction with a scientific reason. For example: practice will shorten reaction time because....

reliable

Results that are similar when repeated. For example: five trials each.

neuronea nerve cell
