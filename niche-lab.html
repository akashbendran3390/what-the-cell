A place and a way of life

A species' niche is its role in the ecosystem: where it lives, what it eats, what eats it, how it gets energy and what conditions it can tolerate. Sort organisms by how they feed, compare the teeth of two hominids, match adaptations of hunters and hunted, and discover why two species cannot share exactly the same niche.
