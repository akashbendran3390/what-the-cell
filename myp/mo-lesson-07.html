Notes: Falling objects

gravity

The force that pulls masses together. For example: the Earth pulls you down.

g

Acceleration due to gravity, about 9.8 m/s2. For example: near the Earth's surface.

free fall

Falling with only gravity acting. For example: on the Moon (no air).

air resistance

The force of air pushing against a moving object. For example: a parachute.

terminal velocity

The steady top speed when forces balance. For example: a skydiver at about 55 m/s.

weight

The force of gravity on a mass (W = mg). For example: a 50 kg student: 490 N.
