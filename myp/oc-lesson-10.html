Notes: Plastics: properties and types

durable

Lasts a long time without breaking down; useful, but also why plastic waste stays in the environment. For example: a bottle can last hundreds of years.

cross-link

A strong bond between chains. For example: why sockets stay hard.

plasticiser

Small molecules that sit between the chains and push them apart, so they slide more easily; the plastic becomes softer and bendier. For example: rigid PVC for pipes; PVC with plasticiser for cable coating and raincoats.

float test

A quick way to sort plastics by density. For example: PP floats; PET sinks.
