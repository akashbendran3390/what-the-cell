Notes: Types of chemical reaction

synthesis

Two or more substances join to make one. For example: iron + sulfur -> iron sulfide.

decomposition

One substance breaks down into two or more. For example: copper carbonate -> copper oxide + carbon dioxide.

displacement

A more reactive element takes the place of a less reactive one. For example: zinc + copper sulfate.

combustion

Burning: a reaction with oxygen that releases heat and light. For example: methane burning.

thermal decomposition

Breaking down using heat. For example: heating limestone.

oxide

A compound of an element with oxygen. For example: magnesium oxide.

limestone

Calcium carbonate, decomposed by heat to make lime for cement. For example: calcium carbonate -> calcium oxide + carbon dioxide.
