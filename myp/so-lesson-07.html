Notes: Thermal energy transfer

conduction

Heat passing through a solid as particles bump into their neighbours. For example: a hot pan handle.

convection

Heat carried by a moving liquid or gas: hot fluid rises, cool fluid sinks. For example: air rising above a heater.

radiation

Heat travelling as infrared waves; it can cross empty space. For example: the Sun warming your face.

conductor

A material that lets heat through easily. For example: metals such as copper.

insulator

A material that slows down heat transfer. For example: wool, plastic, trapped air.

infrared

Invisible waves given off by all warm objects. For example: a thermal camera sees them.

radiation

Transfer of thermal energy by infrared waves. It needs no particles, so it can cross the vacuum of space.. For example: the Sun's heat reaching Earth.

Key words

convectionhot fluid rises, cool fluid sinks; liquids and gases
radiationinfrared waves; works through space
