Practical

Comparing protein sequences between species

Data40 minA4.1.2

You will need: Haemoglobin beta sequences for human and several other species from UniProt, spreadsheet

  1. Line up short sections of the sequence from each species
  2. Count the amino acid differences from the human sequence
  3. Rank the species by number of differences
  4. Discuss what the ranking suggests about common ancestry

Safety: No practical hazards.

Carry this out with your teacher's supervision and follow your school's lab rules.
