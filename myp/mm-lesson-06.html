Notes: Comparing structures

ionic

Positive and negative ions in a lattice. For example: salt.

simple molecular

Small molecules with weak forces between them. For example: sugar, wax, water.

giant covalent

A network of covalently bonded atoms. For example: diamond, sand.

metallic

Metal ions in a sea of electrons. For example: copper, steel.

soluble

Dissolves in a solvent. For example: salt in water.

evidence

Observations and measurements that support a conclusion. For example: a test result.
