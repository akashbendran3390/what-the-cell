Notes: Using chemicals safely

hazard

Something that could cause harm. For example: a flammable liquid.

pictogram

A symbol that shows a hazard. For example: flame, skull, exclamation mark.

toxic

Can poison or kill if swallowed, breathed in or touched. For example: some pesticides.

corrosive

Destroys skin, eyes or metals. For example: drain cleaner.

flammable

Catches fire easily. For example: petrol, paint thinner.

disposal

Getting rid of something safely. For example: battery collection points.
