Notes: Climate change

greenhouse effect

Gases in the air trap some of the Earth's heat, keeping it warm enough for life. For example: without it Earth would be about -18 degrees C.

greenhouse gas

A gas that traps infrared (heat) radiation. For example: carbon dioxide, methane, water vapour.

carbon dioxide (CO2)

Made when fuels burn; the main greenhouse gas we add. For example: car exhaust, power stations.

enhanced greenhouse effect

Extra greenhouse gases from humans trap more heat, so Earth warms. For example: burning fossil fuels.

climate change

Long-term changes in temperature and weather patterns. For example: hotter summers, rising seas.

global warming

The rise in the Earth's average temperature. For example: about 1.2 degrees C since 1850.

enhanced greenhouse effect

Humans add extra greenhouse gases, so more infrared is trapped and the Earth's average temperature rises. For example: burning coal, oil and gas.
