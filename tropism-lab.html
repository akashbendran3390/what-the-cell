Plant Hormone Lab

DP Biology C3.1 (HL) · tropisms, auxin, cytokinin and ethylene

Plants without nerves

A plant cannot move to the light, but it can grow towards it. In this lab you repeat the classic seedling experiments on phototropism, follow auxin from the shoot tip into the cell wall, change the balance of auxin and cytokinin to decide whether a plant makes roots or shoots, and watch one ripe banana set off a whole bowl.

The seedlings are grass coleoptiles (the sheath around a young grass shoot), the plant used in the original experiments. Rates are typical values; real seedlings vary.
