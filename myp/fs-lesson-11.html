Notes: Collisions and safety

collision

When objects crash into each other. For example: a car hitting a wall.

impact force

The force on an object during a collision. For example: bigger when it stops quickly.

helmet

A hard shell with soft padding that protects the head. For example: cycling helmet.

seat belt

A strap that stops you flying forward and spreads the force. For example: in every car.

crumple zone

Part of a car designed to squash in a crash. For example: the bonnet.

airbag

A bag that fills with gas in a crash to cushion you. For example: in the steering wheel.
