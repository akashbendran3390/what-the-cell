Lesson 4: Observing and measuring

What is the difference between what you see and what you think? In this lesson you measure at five stations and investigate a mystery box.

By the end of the lesson you can

  • Distinguish between an observation and an inference
  • State the units for length, volume, mass, temperature and time
  • Read a ruler, measuring cylinder, balance, thermometer and stopwatch
  • Record measurements with the correct unit

Notes: Observing and measuring

observation

What you notice with your senses or an instrument. For example: the water is cloudy.

inference

An explanation you think of from what you observed. For example: someone added milk.

unit

The standard amount a measurement is counted in. For example: metre, gram, second.

volume

How much space something takes up. For example: 50 mL of water.

mass

How much matter is in an object. For example: 200 g.

temperature

How hot or cold something is. For example: 21 degrees Celsius.

meniscus

The curved surface of a liquid in a narrow tube. For example: read the bottom of the curve for water.
