Notes: Pressure in liquids and air

fluid pressure

Caused by the weight of the fluid above pushing on everything below. For example: more water above = more pressure.

atmospheric pressure

The pressure caused by the weight of all the air above; about 101 kPa at sea level. For example: about 10 tonnes on each m2.

Key words

fluidliquid or gas
P = rho g hpressure in a liquid at depth h
depthdistance below the surface, in m
atmospheric pressureabout 101 kPa at sea level
barometermeasures air pressure
altitudeheight above sea level
vacuumalmost no particles: no pressure
pressurised cabinair pumped in to keep pressure safe
