Notes: Designing assistive technology

user-centred design

Designing with and for the people who will use it. For example: asking users what they need.

design brief

A short description of the problem and who it is for. For example: a reacher for someone in a wheelchair.

prototype

A first model to test an idea. For example: a card model.

criteria

What the design must do to succeed. For example: lift 5 N, cost under 100 000 VND.

equity

Fairness: giving each person what they need to take part. For example: ramps as well as stairs.

evaluate

Judge strengths and weaknesses against the criteria. For example: user feedback.
