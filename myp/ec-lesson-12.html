Notes: Review

How to revise

Use the revision deck on the unit page, then the unit notes one section at a time, then the unit review sheet under test conditions. Practise in the Energy Changes Lab.

Key equations

Q = mcΔT; E = V × I × t; energy per gram = Q / mass of fuel burned; ΔH = bonds broken − bonds made; percentage difference = (measured − accepted) / accepted × 100.

Command terms

State: give the fact. Describe: say what happens. Explain: say what happens and why. Calculate: equation, working, unit. Suggest: use science in a new situation. Compare: both, with a similarity or difference.

Calculation checklist

Write the equation; substitute with units; convert units first (g to kg, minutes to seconds); give the answer with a unit; check the size makes sense.

The ten common slips

Mixing heat and temperature; final temperature as ΔT; minutes in E = VIt; fuel mass in Q = mcΔT; heat loss direction; breaking bonds releasing energy; made minus broken; reversed equation with the same sign.

The big idea

Our understanding of chemical reactions and the energy changes that go with them has led to innovative new products in our lives: hand warmers, cold packs, self-heating meals, biofuels and better stoves.
