Notes: Reflection and plane mirrors

reflection

Light bouncing off a surface. For example: a mirror.

normal

A line at 90 degrees to the mirror where the ray hits. For example: dashed line in diagrams.

angle of incidence

Angle between the incoming ray and the normal. For example: i.

angle of reflection

Angle between the reflected ray and the normal. For example: r.

plane mirror

A flat mirror. For example: bathroom mirror.

virtual image

An image that light rays only seem to come from. For example: behind a mirror.

virtual image

An image that cannot be put on a screen; rays only seem to come from it. For example: your reflection.
