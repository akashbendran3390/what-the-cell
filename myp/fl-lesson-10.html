Notes: Helicopters and rotation

rotor

A set of spinning blades that act as wings; the helicopter does not need to move forwards to make lift. For example: about 5 turns every second.

autorotation

Air flowing up through a falling rotor keeps it spinning and slows the fall. For example: a maple seed or a paper helicopter.

Key words

pitch (of a blade)the angle of a rotor blade
hoverlift equals weight
torquea turning effect of a force
