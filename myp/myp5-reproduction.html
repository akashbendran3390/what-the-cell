Reproduction

How living things make the next generation: cloning and cell division, flowers and seeds, the human reproductive systems, pregnancy and birth, and how science helps and protects fertility.

MYP 5, Unit 114 lessonsCriteria A and D

The big picture

Key concept: RelationshipsRelated: PatternsRelated: InteractionsRelated: ConsequencesGlobal context: Identities and relationships

Statement of inquiryNurturing the next generation sustains species.

Factual
How do organisms reproduce? What are the advantages of sexual reproduction? What do sex hormones do?
Conceptual
Where does reproduction fit in the cycle of life? How do embryos develop? Why does infertility happen?
Debatable
Should humans control their fertility? Should societies limit access to assisted reproductive technologies?

Revising for the test

Check you can…

  • compare asexual and sexual reproduction
  • describe binary fission, budding, spores and vegetative propagation
  • describe mitosis (PMAT) and how Dolly was cloned
  • explain meiosis, diploid and haploid, and where variation comes from
  • name the parts of a flower and their jobs
  • tell pollination from fertilisation
  • describe seed dispersal and what germination needs
  • trace the path of sperm
  • describe the 28-day cycle and its four hormones
  • explain how the placenta supplies the foetus
  • tell identical from non-identical twins
  • explain contraception, IVF and how STIs spread

Revise in this order

  1. Work through the revision deck below: try each quick check before you click on.
  2. Read the unit notes, one section at a time, and learn the words in bold.
  3. Do the unit review sheet under test conditions, then check it with your teacher.
  4. Practise in the Reproduction Lab and the Flower and Seed Lab.
