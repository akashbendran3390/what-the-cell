Energy flows, matter cycles

Energy enters most ecosystems as sunlight, passes along food chains and leaves as heat. Matter, such as carbon, is recycled again and again. Explore a food web, build an energy pyramid, run the global carbon cycle, and read the most famous graph in climate science.
