Lesson 7: Lighter than air

How can a balloon lift people without wings? In this lesson you launch a bin-bag hot-air balloon and calculate the forces on balloons.

MYP 5, Unit 6Lesson 7 of 1555 minutes
← Lesson 6: Floating and sinkingAll lessonsLesson 8: Heavier than air →

By the end of the lesson you can

  • Explain why heating air makes a balloon rise
  • Calculate the upthrust, weight and resultant force on a balloon
  • Compare hot air, helium and hydrogen for lifting
  • Describe how balloons and airships changed flight

Notes: Lighter than air

average density of a balloon

The mass of the envelope, basket, people and hot air, divided by the balloon's volume. For example: less than 1.29 kg/m3: it rises.
