What is happening

In dilute solutions the potato cells contain more solute than the solution, so water enters by osmosis and the cylinders gain mass. In concentrated solutions water leaves and they lose mass. Where there is no change, the solution and the cell contents are isotonic.

Water moves from higher to lower water potential. Adding solute lowers water potential, so pure water (ψ = 0 kPa) has the highest possible value.
