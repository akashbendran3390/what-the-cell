Notes: Velocity-time graphs

velocity-time graph

Velocity (y) against time (x). For example: a train between stations.

gradient

Rise / run; here it is the acceleration. For example: steeper = bigger acceleration.

area under the graph

The space between the line and the time axis. For example: equals the distance.

constant velocity

A flat line on a velocity-time graph. For example: cruise control.

uniform acceleration

Acceleration that stays the same. For example: a straight sloping line.

triangle area

Half × base × height. For example: for speeding up from rest.
