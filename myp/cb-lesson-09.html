Notes: Homeostasis and body temperature

homeostasis

Keeping conditions inside the body steady. For example: body temperature about 37 degrees C.

negative feedback

A change triggers a response that reverses it. For example: too hot -> sweat -> cooler.

vasodilation

Blood vessels near the skin widen to lose heat. For example: red face after exercise.

vasoconstriction

Blood vessels near the skin narrow to keep heat. For example: pale, cold fingers.

sweating

Sweat evaporates and cools the skin. For example: on a hot day.

shivering

Muscles contract quickly to release heat. For example: in cold water.
