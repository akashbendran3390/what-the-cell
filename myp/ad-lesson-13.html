Notes: Human impact on ecosystems

extinction

When a species dies out completely. For example: dodo, 1681.

endangered

At high risk of extinction. For example: pangolin.

invasive species

A species brought to a new place that spreads and causes harm. For example: cane toad in Australia.

habitat loss

Destruction of the places organisms live. For example: deforestation.

biodiversity

The variety of life in an area. For example: high in rainforests.

conservation

Protecting species and habitats. For example: national parks.

captive breeding

Breeding endangered animals in zoos or centres and releasing them. For example: bison back from near extinction.
