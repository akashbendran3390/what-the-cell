Notes: States of matter

matter

Anything that has mass and takes up space. For example: water, air, rock.

solid

Keeps its own shape and volume. For example: ice, gold, wood.

liquid

Takes the shape of its container but keeps its volume. For example: water, oil, honey.

gas

Spreads out to fill any container. For example: air, steam.

compress

Squash into a smaller volume. For example: pumping air into a tyre.

plasma

A very hot state of matter with charged particles. For example: the Sun, lightning.
