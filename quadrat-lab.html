DP Biology A4.2 · invented sites for practice

Sample two sites and compare their biodiversity

Two fields seen from above: a flower-rich meadow and a field that has been heavily fertilised and grazed. Place quadrats, count the plants of each species inside, and calculate Simpson’s reciprocal index to compare how diverse the sites are.

Click anywhere on the field to place a quadrat yourself. Each quadrat is 1 m by 1 m.

Try it

  1. Place quadrats only where you like the look of the field. Then clear and use random quadrats. Why should sampling be random?
  2. How many quadrats do you need before the index stops changing much?
  3. Both sites have the same species. Compare richness and evenness, and explain why the index is different.
  4. Suggest which human activities could turn a site like A into a site like B.
