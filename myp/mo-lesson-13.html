Lesson 13: Car safety features

Why are modern cars designed to crumple? In this lesson you design a crumple zone to protect an egg and discuss why cars are not even safer.

Notes: Car safety features

momentum

Mass × velocity (kg m/s). For example: a heavy truck at speed has a lot.

crumple zone

The front or back of a car that is designed to crush slowly. For example: the bonnet folds up.

airbag

A bag that inflates in a crash to slow the head and chest gently. For example: in the steering wheel.

collision time

How long a crash takes to stop you. For example: longer is safer.

helmet

A shell and foam liner that crushes to protect the head. For example: motorbike and bicycle helmets.

safety cage

The strong frame around the passengers. For example: does not crumple.
