How a cell hears a message

A signalling chemical only affects cells that have a matching receptor. Step through five ways a signal gets into action: bacteria counting each other, a neurotransmitter opening a channel, adrenaline switching on a G protein, insulin activating a tyrosine kinase, and a steroid hormone changing which genes are expressed.
