Notes: Polyatomic ions

polyatomic ion

An ion made of a group of atoms joined together, with an overall charge. For example: sulfate SO42-.

sulfate

SO42-. For example: in Epsom salts.

nitrate

NO3^-. For example: in fertilisers.

carbonate

CO32-. For example: in limescale and chalk.

hydroxide

OH^-. For example: in drain cleaner.

ammonium

NH4^+, the only common positive one. For example: in fertilisers.
