Notes: Crude oil and fractional distillation

fractional distillation

Separating crude oil into fractions by boiling point in a column with a temperature gradient. For example: done in every oil refinery.

intermolecular forces

Weak forces between molecules; longer molecules attract each other more, so more energy is needed to separate them. For example: why bitumen boils so high.

feedstock

A raw material used to make other chemicals. For example: naphtha for plastics.
