Notes: Plastics and sustainability

non-biodegradable

Not broken down by living things; the same property that makes plastic useful (durable, unreactive) makes it a long-lasting pollutant. For example: a bottle on a beach.

bioaccumulation

A substance building up in living things faster than it is removed, so it increases up a food chain. For example: toxins on microplastics.

bioplastic

A plastic made from plants or one that biodegrades. For example: PLA cups from corn starch.

Key words

biodegradablebroken down by microorganisms
non-biodegradablenot broken down; lasts for centuries
microplasticsplastic pieces under 5 mm
recyclingturning used materials into new products
landfillburying waste
bioplasticmade from plants or able to biodegrade
sustainabilitymeeting needs without harming the future
