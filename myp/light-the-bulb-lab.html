Light the Bulb Lab

One cell, wires and a bulb. Find out what a bulb needs before it will light, test your ideas about current, and sort conductors from insulators.

Your challenge

  1. Drag the coloured ends of the two wires onto the cell and the bulb.
  2. Make the bulb light. Then find a second, different way.
  3. Try touching a wire on the glass, or joining + to −. What happens?
