Notes: Sound, noise and materials

noise

Unwanted or unpleasant sound. For example: traffic at night.

decibel (dB)

The unit for sound level. For example: normal talk about 60 dB.

sound level meter

A device that measures sound level in dB. For example: phone apps.

absorb

Take in sound energy so less is reflected. For example: foam, curtains.

reflect

Bounce sound back. For example: hard walls.

noise pollution

Harmful or annoying levels of noise. For example: construction at night.
