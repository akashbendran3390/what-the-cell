Energy Changes Lab

Mix hot and cold water, heat metal blocks, sort reactions into exothermic and endothermic, burn fuels under a calorimeter, and work out energy changes from bond energies and Hess’s law.

Breaking bonds takes in energy; making bonds gives out energy. ΔH = energy to break bonds − energy released making bonds. Choose a reaction, count the bonds in the displayed formulae and fill in the three boxes.

Draw the displayed formulae and count every bond. Most mistakes are miscounted bonds.

Hess’s law: the total energy change is the same whichever route the reaction takes. Reverse or multiply the given equations so that they add up to the target equation. Reversing changes the sign of ΔH; multiplying multiplies ΔH.
