Notes: Newton's second law

Newton's second law

Resultant force = mass × acceleration. For example: F = ma.

resultant force (F)

The overall force, in newtons. For example: 2000 N forwards.

mass (m)

In kilograms. For example: a 1200 kg car.

acceleration (a)

In m/s2. For example: 2 m/s2.

weight (W)

The force of gravity on a mass: W = mg. For example: a 60 kg person weighs 588 N.

gravitational field strength (g)

9.8 N/kg on Earth, 1.6 N/kg on the Moon. For example: the same as 9.8 m/s2.
