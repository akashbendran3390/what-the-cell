Notes: Diffusion

diffusion

Particles spreading from where there are many to where there are few. For example: smell spreading through a room.

concentration

How many particles there are in a space. For example: strong orange juice has a high concentration.

random

In no fixed direction or pattern. For example: particles bumping in all directions.

evenly spread

The same concentration everywhere. For example: dye fully mixed.

rate

How fast something happens. For example: faster in hot water.

scent

A substance that we smell. For example: perfume, coffee.
