Notes: Covalent bonding

covalent bond

A shared pair of electrons between two non-metal atoms. For example: H-H in hydrogen.

molecule

Two or more atoms joined by covalent bonds. For example: H2O.

shared pair

Two electrons held between two atoms. For example: one from each atom.

dot-and-cross diagram

Shows the electrons of each atom as dots or crosses. For example: for HCl.

outer shell

The shell that takes part in bonding. For example: full with 8 (2 for hydrogen).

displayed formula

Shows every atom and every bond as a line. For example: H-O-H.
