Practical

Surface area, volume and diffusion

Lab40 minB2.3.6

You will need: Agar cubes of three sizes containing a pH indicator, dilute hydrochloric acid, ruler, timer

  1. Calculate the surface area-to-volume ratio of each cube
  2. Place the cubes in dilute acid
  3. Time how long each takes to change colour all the way through
  4. Plot time against ratio, and relate this to limits on cell size

Safety: Eye protection. Use acid no stronger than 0.1 M.

Carry this out with your teacher's supervision and follow your school's lab rules.
