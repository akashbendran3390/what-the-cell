Notes: Hydraulics and pneumatics

pressure

Force per unit area. For example: P = F / A, in pascals (Pa) or N/cm2.

hydraulics

Using liquids under pressure to transmit force. For example: car brakes, lifts.

pneumatics

Using air under pressure to transmit force. For example: air brakes, sip-and-puff.

piston

A sliding plug in a cylinder. For example: in a syringe.

incompressible

Cannot be squashed. For example: liquids.

sip-and-puff switch

Sends signals by breathing in or out through a tube. For example: controls a wheelchair.

pneumatic switch

A switch worked by a puff or sip of air: pressure changes trigger it. For example: used by people who cannot use their hands.
