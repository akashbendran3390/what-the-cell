Notes: Diet, exercise and sleep

energy

What food gives us; measured in kJ. For example: about 10 000 kJ a day for a teenager.

pulse rate

The number of heartbeats per minute. For example: about 60 to 100 at rest.

recovery time

How long the pulse takes to return to resting. For example: shorter when fitter.

aerobic exercise

Exercise that makes you breathe harder for a long time. For example: running, swimming.

obesity

Having too much body fat for good health. For example: linked to type 2 diabetes.

sleep

Rest when the brain sorts memories and the body grows. For example: 8 to 10 hours for teenagers.

body clock

The internal timer that makes us sleepy; in teenagers it shifts later. For example: feeling awake at 11 pm.
