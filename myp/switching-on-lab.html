Switching On Lab

Build energy chains, draw Sankey diagrams, run up an electricity bill, heat particles, race heat along rods, plan a country’s electricity, run a power station, wind an electromagnet, spin a generator and turn up the greenhouse effect.

All matter is made of particles that are always moving. Heat the substance: the particles move faster (higher temperature) and further apart (expansion). Keep heating and it melts, then boils.

Thermal energy travels by conduction (particles bump into neighbours), convection (warm fluid rises, cool fluid sinks) and radiation (infrared waves, even through a vacuum).

Challenge. Which way of transferring heat is stopped by the vacuum in a flask? (choose all)
