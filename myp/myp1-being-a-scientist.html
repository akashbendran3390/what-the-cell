Being a Scientist

What scientists do and how science grew across many cultures, how to work safely in the lab, how to observe and measure, and how to plan a fair test, record results, draw graphs and write a conclusion, ending with your own pendulum investigation.

MYP 1, Unit 112 lessonsCriteria A, B, C and D

The big picture

Key concept: RelationshipsRelated: EvidenceRelated: PatternsRelated: DevelopmentGlobal context: Scientific and technical innovation

Statement of inquiryScientists look for patterns and collect evidence through fair tests to explain how things in the world are related.

Factual
What are the steps of a scientific investigation? What are independent, dependent and control variables? How do we draw a line graph?
Conceptual
Why does a fair test change only one variable? Why do scientists repeat measurements? How do scientific ideas develop over time?
Debatable
Can science answer every question? Should we trust a claim just because it sounds scientific?

Revising for the test

Check you can…

  • describe what scientists do and give examples of different sciences
  • outline how science developed in different cultures and why theories change
  • name common lab equipment and follow lab safety rules and hazard symbols
  • measure length, volume, mass, temperature and time with the right units
  • write a testable question and a hypothesis with a reason
  • identify the independent, dependent and control variables in a fair test
  • write a clear method and a results table with units, and calculate a mean
  • choose and draw a bar chart or line graph with a line of best fit, and spot anomalies
  • write a conclusion and an evaluation with improvements
  • plan and carry out an investigation on your own
  • tell science from pseudoscience and describe how science helps the world

Revise in this order

  1. Read the unit notes, one section at a time, and learn the words in bold.
  2. Open each lesson and look through its slides, then redo Section C of its worksheet.
  3. Practise with the Scientist Lab: try the challenge at the bottom of each tab.
  4. Do the unit review sheet under test conditions, then try the sample Criterion A paper.
