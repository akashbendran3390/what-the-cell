Practical

Plotting hormones across the menstrual cycle

Data35 minD3.1.5

You will need: Published FSH, LH, oestradiol and progesterone values from a textbook, graph paper or spreadsheet

  1. Plot all four hormones against day of the cycle on one graph
  2. Mark ovulation and menstruation
  3. Annotate where each hormone rises and falls
  4. Explain two feedback links that the graph shows

Safety: No practical hazards. Handle the topic sensitively.

Carry this out with your teacher's supervision and follow your school's lab rules.
