Notes: Pedigree charts

the key clue

Two unaffected parents with an affected child prove the allele is recessive; two affected parents with an unaffected child prove it is dominant. For example: look for these families first.

genetic test

A test of a person's DNA for a particular allele. For example: testing for the cystic fibrosis allele.

Key words

pedigree charta family tree showing a condition
generationone row of a pedigree: I, II, III
affectedshows the condition; filled symbol
autosomalon chromosomes 1–22
carrierhas one recessive allele but is unaffected
genetic counsellorexplains inheritance risks to families
