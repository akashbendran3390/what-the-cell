Lesson 1: What is flight?

What force must every flying thing overcome? In this lesson you time falling objects and draw free-body diagrams to find the resultant force.

Notes: What is flight?

gravitational field strength

How hard gravity pulls on each kilogram: 9.8 N/kg on Earth, 1.6 N/kg on the Moon. For example: g = 9.8 N/kg.

newton (N)

The unit of force; about the weight of a small apple. For example: a 100 g apple weighs about 1 N.

Key words

forcea push or a pull, in newtons (N)
massamount of matter, in kg; the same everywhere
weightpull of gravity on a mass, W = mg, in N
ggravitational field strength: 9.8 N/kg on Earth
free-body diagramone object with an arrow for each force
resultant forceall the forces added together
balanced forcesresultant zero; no change in motion
terminal velocitysteady falling speed when drag = weight
