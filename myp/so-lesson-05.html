Lesson 5: Paying for electricity

How much does it cost to run an air conditioner all night? In this lesson you read a meter, work out a bill and audit the energy use of a home.

Notes: Paying for electricity

kilowatt (kW)

1000 watts. For example: a 1.5 kW air conditioner.

kilowatt-hour (kWh)

The energy used by a 1 kW appliance in 1 hour; the unit on electricity bills. For example: a 1 kW iron used for 1 hour.

electricity meter

A device that counts the kWh a home uses. For example: the meter outside your home.

unit price

The cost of 1 kWh. For example: about 2500 VND per kWh.

standby

An appliance switched off with a remote but still using power. For example: a TV with its red light on.

energy audit

A check of where a home uses energy and how to save it. For example: listing every appliance.

kilowatt-hour (kWh)

The energy transferred by a 1 kW appliance in 1 hour. 1 kWh = 3 600 000 J.. For example: a 2 kW heater for 3 hours uses 6 kWh.
