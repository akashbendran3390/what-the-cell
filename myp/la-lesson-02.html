Notes: Hormones and the endocrine system

endocrine system

The glands that release hormones into the blood. For example: pituitary, thyroid, ovaries.

gland

An organ that makes and releases a substance. For example: sweat gland, thyroid.

pituitary gland

The master gland under the brain. For example: releases FSH and LH.

target organ

The organ a hormone acts on. For example: ovaries for FSH.

FSH

Follicle-stimulating hormone: starts eggs or sperm developing. For example: from the pituitary.

testosterone

The main male sex hormone. For example: made in the testes.
