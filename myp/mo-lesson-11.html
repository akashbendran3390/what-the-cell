Notes: Newton's third law

Newton's third law

When A pushes B, B pushes A with an equal force in the opposite direction. For example: push a wall, it pushes back.

force pair

The two forces of the third law, on two different objects. For example: foot on ground, ground on foot.

thrust

The forward force from an engine or a rocket. For example: jet engine.

interaction

Two objects acting on each other. For example: a bat and a ball.

exhaust gas

Hot gas pushed out of a rocket. For example: pushed down and back.

reaction force

The second force in a pair. For example: the ground pushing up.
