Notes: Active transport and surface area

active transport

Moving particles against the concentration gradient, using energy. For example: root hairs taking in minerals.

carrier protein

A protein in the membrane that carries particles across. For example: the sodium-potassium pump.

energy (ATP)

Released by respiration in mitochondria. For example: powers active transport.

surface area

The total area of the outside. For example: the six faces of a cube.

volume

The space something takes up. For example: cm3.

exchange surface

A surface adapted for taking in or giving out substances. For example: the lungs, the gut.

active transport

Moving particles from a low to a high concentration (against the gradient), using energy from respiration. For example: minerals into root hairs.
