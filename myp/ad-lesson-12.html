Notes: Selective breeding and genetic modification

artificial selection

Humans choose which organisms breed. For example: also called selective breeding.

selective breeding

Breeding the individuals with the features we want. For example: high-yield wheat.

genetic modification

Changing an organism's genes directly in a lab. For example: golden rice.

yield

The amount of food produced. For example: tonnes of rice per hectare.

trait

A feature that can be inherited. For example: fur colour, height.

inbreeding

Breeding closely related individuals. For example: can cause health problems.

golden rice

Rice with genes added so it makes beta-carotene (vitamin A). For example: helps prevent childhood blindness.
