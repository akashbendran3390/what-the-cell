Notes: The digestive system

digestion

Breaking large food molecules into small ones the body can absorb. For example: starch into sugar.

mechanical digestion

Breaking food into smaller pieces physically. For example: chewing; stomach churning.

chemical digestion

Enzymes breaking large molecules into small ones. For example: amylase breaks starch.

oesophagus

The tube from the mouth to the stomach. For example: food is squeezed down it.

absorption

Small food molecules passing into the blood. For example: in the small intestine.

peristalsis

Waves of muscle squeezing food along the gut. For example: like toothpaste in a tube.
