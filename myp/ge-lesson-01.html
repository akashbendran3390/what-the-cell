Notes: DNA, genes and chromosomes

gene

A section of DNA that holds the instructions for one feature. Most genes do this by coding for one protein (Lesson 3).. For example: one gene affects whether pea seeds are round or wrinkled.

karyotype

All the chromosomes from one cell, photographed and sorted into pairs by size. For example: used to check a baby's chromosomes.

genome

All the DNA of an organism. Every body cell holds a full copy.. For example: printed out, it would fill about a million pages.

specialised cell

A cell that uses only some of its genes, so it has a special shape and job. For example: nerve, muscle, red blood cell.

precipitate

To come out of a solution as a solid. For example: DNA in cold alcohol.

diploid / haploid

Two sets of chromosomes (body cells) / one set (gametes). For example: human: 46 / 23.

Key words

DNAthe molecule that carries genetic instructions
genea section of DNA that codes for one feature
chromosomea long coiled DNA molecule; 46 in human body cells
genomeall the DNA of an organism
karyotypea picture of all the chromosomes sorted into pairs
diploid / haploidtwo sets / one set of chromosomes
sex chromosomesX and Y: XX female, XY male
