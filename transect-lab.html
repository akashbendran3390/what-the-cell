Salt Marsh Transect Lab

DP Biology B4.1 · an invented marsh with realistic patterns

Why does each plant grow where it does?

A salt marsh slopes up from the sea to dry land. Near the sea, plants are flooded by salt water twice a day; higher up they are hardly ever flooded. Lay a transect from the sea to the land, sample with quadrats along it, and build a kite diagram to see how each species is limited by how much flooding and salt it can tolerate.
