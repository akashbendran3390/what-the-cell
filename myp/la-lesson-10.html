Notes: Alcohol and drugs

drug

A substance that changes how the body or mind works. For example: caffeine, medicines, alcohol.

depressant

A drug that slows the nervous system. For example: alcohol.

addiction

A strong need to keep taking a drug. For example: nicotine, opioids.

binge drinking

Drinking a lot of alcohol in a short time. For example: can cause alcohol poisoning.

opioid

A strong painkiller drug that can stop breathing in overdose. For example: fentanyl, heroin.

liver

The organ that breaks down alcohol. For example: about one drink per hour.

alcohol poisoning

So much alcohol that the brain stem slows breathing and heart rate dangerously. For example: call emergency help; never leave the person alone.
