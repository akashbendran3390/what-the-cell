Keeping ecosystems going

Natural ecosystems can stay much the same for centuries, but push them too far and they can change suddenly. Clear a rainforest, remove a keystone predator, overfish a sea, wash fertiliser into a lake or poison a food chain, and see what happens, then try putting things right.
