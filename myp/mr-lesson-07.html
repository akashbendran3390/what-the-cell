Notes: Electricity from metals and electroplating

cell

Two different metals in an electrolyte that make a voltage. For example: a lemon battery.

electrolyte

A liquid that conducts because it contains ions. For example: lemon juice, salt water.

electrode

A metal or carbon rod that touches the electrolyte. For example: zinc and copper strips.

voltage

The push that makes current flow, in volts (V). For example: about 0.9 V for zinc and copper.

electroplating

Using electricity to coat an object with a thin layer of metal. For example: silver-plated cutlery.

battery

Two or more cells joined together. For example: a car battery.

plating

A thin coat of metal on another metal. For example: gold on electrical contacts.
