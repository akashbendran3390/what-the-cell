Notes: Body image and healthy eating

body image

How a person sees and feels about their own body. For example: can be positive or negative.

balanced diet

Eating the right amounts of each nutrient. For example: half a plate of vegetables and fruit.

carbohydrate

The main energy nutrient. For example: rice, bread, noodles.

protein

Nutrient for growth and repair. For example: fish, eggs, beans.

anorexia

An eating disorder where a person eats far too little. For example: a serious illness.

food label

The nutrition facts printed on a packet. For example: energy in kJ per 100 g.

per 100 g

The amount in 100 grams, so you can compare foods fairly. For example: sugar 22 g per 100 g = 22%.
