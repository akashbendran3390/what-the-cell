Notes: Simple molecules and their properties

double bond

Two shared pairs between two atoms. For example: O=O in oxygen.

triple bond

Three shared pairs. For example: N in nitrogen gas.

intermolecular forces

Weak forces between molecules. For example: broken when wax melts.

simple molecular

Made of small separate molecules. For example: water, carbon dioxide, wax.

volatile

Evaporates easily. For example: petrol.

insulator

Does not conduct electricity. For example: plastic, wax.
