Notes: What is science?

science

Finding out how the world works by careful observation and testing. For example: why does ice melt?.

scientist

A person who asks questions and tests ideas with evidence. For example: a marine biologist.

observation

Noticing something with your senses or an instrument. For example: the sky is grey.

evidence

Facts or results that show whether an idea is right. For example: measurements of rainfall.

discovery

Finding something new that nobody knew before. For example: bacteria in 1676.

technology

Using science to make useful things. For example: vaccines, phones.

evidence

Facts, observations or measurements that show whether an idea is right or wrong. For example: a thermometer reading of 38 degrees shows a fever.
