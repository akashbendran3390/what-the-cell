Topic questions

A generator that builds a fresh set of practice questions for any topic and level, with answers. It is on its way.

Coming soon. The topic question generator is being built. Until it is ready, use the practice you already have on the site, listed below.

What it will do

  • Pick a topic: any SL or AHL subtopic, a whole unit, or a mix for revision.
  • Pick a level and type: SL or HL; multiple choice, short answer, data-based or extended response.
  • Get a fresh set: a new mix of questions every time, written for this site in the style of the exam.
  • Check your work: answers and mark points to compare with, so you can practise on your own.

Practise now

  • Self-check quizzes: every subtopic page has a quick self-check with answers. Find your subtopic on the course map.
  • Worksheets: each subtopic page has a worksheet to print.
  • Data questions: graphs and tables to analyse, by unit, like Paper 1B and Paper 2 Section A.
  • Exams and command terms: how each paper works, and what each command term asks you to do.
  • Think deeper: concept challenges and big questions to stretch your understanding.
