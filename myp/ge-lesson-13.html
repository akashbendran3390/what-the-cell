Notes: Review

How to revise

Use the revision deck on the Revision tab, then the unit notes one section at a time, then the unit review sheet under test conditions.

Five lines for every cross

Key, parents, gametes, grid, ratio. Give probabilities as 1 in 4, 0.25 and 25%.

Command terms

State: give the fact. Describe: say what happens. Explain: say what happens and why. Deduce: work it out from evidence. Discuss: give both sides and a conclusion.

The big idea

Patterns in how genes are passed on help us understand families, other living things and the choices science gives us.

Key words

genotype / phenotypethe alleles / the feature they produce
homozygous / heterozygoustwo same / two different alleles
codominanceboth alleles show fully
