Notes: Species and scientific names

species

Organisms that can breed together to produce fertile offspring. For example: all pet cats.

fertile

Able to have offspring. For example: a horse foal grows up and can breed.

hybrid

Offspring of two different species, often infertile. For example: a mule.

binomial name

A two-word scientific name: genus then species. For example: Panthera tigris.

endangered

At risk of dying out. For example: the saola.

extinct

No longer exists anywhere. For example: the dodo.
