Notes: Groups of metals

alkali metals

Group 1: soft, very reactive metals. For example: lithium, sodium, potassium.

alkaline earth metals

Group 2: reactive but less than group 1. For example: magnesium, calcium.

transition metals

The block in the middle: hard, strong, less reactive. For example: iron, copper, gold.

reactive

Reacts quickly and easily. For example: potassium in water.

noble gases

Group 0: very unreactive gases. For example: helium, neon.

halogens

Group 7: reactive non-metals. For example: chlorine, iodine.

noble gas

A group 0 element with a full outer shell, so it hardly reacts. For example: neon in signs, argon in light bulbs.
