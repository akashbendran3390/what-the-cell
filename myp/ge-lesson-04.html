Notes: Variation

environment

Everything outside your genes: food, sunlight, exercise, illness, upbringing. For example: better food gives taller wheat.

nature and nurture

Nature = what your genes give you; nurture = what your surroundings do. For example: twin studies compare both.

Key words

variationdifferences between individuals of a species
genetic variationcaused by the genes you inherit
environmental variationcaused by surroundings and lifestyle
continuous variationa range of values, measured
discontinuous variationseparate groups, counted
histogramgraph for continuous data; bars touch
identical twinstwo people with the same genes
