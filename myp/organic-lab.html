Organic Molecules Lab

Build alkanes, alkenes and alkynes and name them, explore a fractionating column, sort isomers, crack long molecules, grow polymer chains and identify plastics by floating them.

Choose how many carbons and the type of carbon–carbon bond. The lab draws the displayed formula, gives the molecular formula and the name, and checks it against the general formula. With a double bond you can try addition reactions.

Choose a long alkane and decide how many carbons go into the alkane product. The rest must become an alkene. The lab checks that the atoms balance.

Choose a monomer. In addition polymerisation the C=C bonds open and the monomers join into a chain with nothing else made. Switch to condensation to see a small molecule given off at every link.
