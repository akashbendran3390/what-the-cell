Notes: Forces and resultant force

force

A push or a pull, measured in newtons (N). For example: kicking a ball.

newton meter

A spring balance that measures force. For example: a 10 N meter.

contact force

A force that needs touching. For example: friction, air resistance.

non-contact force

A force that acts at a distance. For example: gravity, magnetism.

free-body diagram

A drawing of one object with arrows for every force. For example: a car with four arrows.

resultant force

The single force that has the same effect as all the forces. For example: 600 N - 400 N = 200 N.

Key words
