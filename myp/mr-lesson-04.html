Notes: Alloys

alloy

A mixture of a metal with other elements. For example: steel, brass, bronze.

steel

Iron with a little carbon. For example: bridges, cars.

bronze

Copper with tin. For example: Dong Son drums, medals.

brass

Copper with zinc. For example: instruments, taps.

stainless steel

Steel with chromium that does not rust. For example: sinks, cutlery.

solder

An alloy that melts easily to join wires. For example: tin with copper or silver.

property

A feature of a material, such as hardness or melting point. For example: solder has a low melting point.
