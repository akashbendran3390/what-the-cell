Notes: Review

adaptation

Feature helping survival and reproduction. For example: thick fur.

food web

Linked food chains. For example: in a forest.

transpiration

Water vapour loss from leaves. For example: faster in wind.

natural selection

Best suited survive and reproduce. For example: peppered moth.

fossil

Preserved remains in rock. For example: Archaeopteryx.

invasive species

Spreads and harms a new place. For example: cane toad.
