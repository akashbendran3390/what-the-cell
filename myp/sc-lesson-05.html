Notes: Questions and hypotheses

research question

The question an investigation tries to answer. For example: how does sugar affect gummy bear growth?.

testable

Can be answered by measuring or observing. For example: does salt water boil faster?.

hypothesis

A prediction with a scientific reason. For example: more sugar, less growth, because....

prediction

What you think will happen. For example: the bear will double in length.

investigation

A planned test to answer a question. For example: the gummy bear investigation.

pseudoscience

Claims that sound scientific but have no good evidence. For example: a lucky bracelet that cures colds.

hypothesis

A prediction you can test, with a scientific reason.
