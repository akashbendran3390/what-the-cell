Lesson 2: Density

Why does a huge iceberg float but a small pebble sink? In this lesson you measure the density of blocks and odd shapes and predict what floats.

Notes: Density

g/cm3 and kg/m3

Two units for density: 1 g/cm3 is the same as 1000 kg/m3. For example: water: 1 g/cm3 = 1000 kg/m3.

average density

The total mass of the object divided by its total volume, including the air inside. For example: a ship: 50 000 t, 70 000 m3: about 710 kg/m3.

Key words

densitymass per unit volume: rho = m / V
volumespace taken up, in cm3 or m3
displacementvolume found from the water pushed aside
meniscusthe curved surface of water in a cylinder
floatless dense than the liquid
sinkdenser than the liquid
average densitytotal mass / total volume, air included
