DP Biology D3.1 · sexual reproduction in flowering plants

From flower to seedling

Follow pollen from one flower to another, grow a pollen tube down to an ovule and watch the flower turn into a fruit. Then find out why plants go to so much trouble to avoid pollinating themselves, scatter seeds as far as they can, and test what a seed needs before it will germinate.
