Notes: Light

luminous

Gives out its own light. For example: the Sun, a candle.

non-luminous

Does not give out light; we see it by reflected light. For example: the Moon, a book.

ray

A straight line with an arrow showing the path of light. For example: in diagrams.

opaque

Lets no light through; makes shadows. For example: wood, metal.

translucent

Lets some light through but scatters it. For example: frosted glass.

transparent

Lets light through clearly. For example: clear glass, water.
