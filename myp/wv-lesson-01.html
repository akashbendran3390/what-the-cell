Notes: What are waves?

wave

A vibration that transfers energy from place to place. For example: water ripples.

amplitude

The height of a wave from the middle line to a crest. For example: bigger for louder sounds.

wavelength

The distance from one crest to the next. For example: measured in metres.

frequency

The number of waves passing a point each second. For example: measured in hertz (Hz).

crest

The top of a wave. For example: highest point.

medium

The material a wave travels through. For example: water, air, rock.

wave speed

Speed = frequency × wavelength (v = f × wavelength). For example: 2 Hz × 3 m = 6 m/s.
