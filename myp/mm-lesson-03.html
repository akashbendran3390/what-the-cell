Notes: Giant covalent structures

giant covalent structure

Millions of atoms joined by covalent bonds in a network. For example: diamond, sand.

diamond

Carbon with every atom bonded to 4 others. For example: hardest natural substance.

graphite

Carbon in layers; each atom bonded to 3 others. For example: pencil lead.

graphene

A single layer of graphite. For example: one atom thick.

silica

Silicon dioxide, SiO2. For example: sand, quartz.

allotrope

A different form of the same element. For example: diamond and graphite.
