Lesson 3: Pressure

Why do snowshoes stop you sinking? In this lesson you find the pressure you put on the floor and see how a hydraulic jack multiplies a force.

Notes: Pressure

pressure

The force acting on each square metre of a surface. For example: 20 N on 0.02 m2 gives 1000 Pa.

Key words

pressureforce per unit area: P = F / A
pascal (Pa)1 N/m2
kilopascal (kPa)1000 Pa
areasurface size in m2 (1 m2 = 10 000 cm2)
hydraulicsliquid passing on pressure to multiply a force
pistona plug that slides in a cylinder
