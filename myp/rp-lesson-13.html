Notes: Review

How to revise

Use the revision deck on the Revision tab, then the unit notes one section at a time, then the unit review sheet under test conditions.

Key numbers

46 chromosomes in a body cell, 23 in a gamete. Mitosis makes 2 identical cells; meiosis makes 4 different ones. Ovulation about day 14 of 28. Full term about 40 weeks.

Command terms

State: give the fact. Describe: say what happens. Explain: say what happens and why. Outline: the main points. Suggest: use science in a new situation.

The big idea

Nurturing the next generation sustains species: food stores in seeds, the placenta, milk and years of parental care.

Key words

meiosismakes four different haploid gametes
pollinationpollen moved from anther to stigma
