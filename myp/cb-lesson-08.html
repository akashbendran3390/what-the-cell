Notes: Hormones and the endocrine system

hormone

A chemical messenger carried in the blood. For example: insulin.

gland

An organ that makes and releases a substance. For example: the pancreas.

endocrine system

All the glands that release hormones into the blood. For example: pituitary, thyroid, adrenal....

target organ

An organ that responds to a hormone. For example: the liver responds to insulin.

pituitary gland

A small gland under the brain that controls other glands. For example: the master gland.

adrenal glands

Glands on the kidneys that make adrenaline. For example: fight or flight.
