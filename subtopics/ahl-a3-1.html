Practical

Making a dichotomous key

Field45 minA3.1.14

You will need: Six to eight local plants or leaves, photographs, ruler

  1. Collect or photograph local specimens
  2. Choose a visible feature that divides them into two groups
  3. Keep dividing until each specimen is identified
  4. Test your key on a classmate, and improve it

Safety: Avoid plants that irritate skin, and do not pick protected plants. Wash hands afterwards.

Carry this out with your teacher's supervision and follow your school's lab rules.
