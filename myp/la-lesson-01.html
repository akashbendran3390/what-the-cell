Notes: Adolescence and puberty

adolescence

The years between childhood and adulthood. For example: about 10 to 19 years old.

puberty

The body changes that make a person able to reproduce. For example: growth spurt.

hormone

A chemical messenger carried in the blood. For example: oestrogen, testosterone.

growth spurt

A fast increase in height. For example: often 6 to 10 cm a year.

emotional health

How we handle feelings. For example: coping with stress.

social health

How we get on with other people. For example: friendships.

pituitary gland

A pea-sized gland under the brain that starts puberty by releasing hormones. For example: FSH and LH.
