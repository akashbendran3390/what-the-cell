Notes: Cracking

cracking products

A shorter alkane (useful as fuel) and an alkene (useful for making plastics and other chemicals). For example: C12H26 -> C10H22 + C2H4.

economic benefit

Cracking turns low-value heavy oil into high-value petrol and plastics. For example: more products from each barrel.

Key words

crackingbreaking long hydrocarbons into shorter ones
supply and demandwhat is made compared with what is wanted
catalystspeeds up a reaction without being used up
catalytic crackingabout 500 °C over a zeolite catalyst
thermal crackingabout 800 °C, with steam
etheneC2H4, raw material for plastics
suck-backcold water rushing into a hot tube
