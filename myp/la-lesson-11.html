Notes: The lungs and breathing

respiratory system

The organs that take in oxygen and remove carbon dioxide. For example: nose, trachea, lungs.

trachea

The windpipe, held open by rings of cartilage. For example: about 12 cm long.

bronchi

The two tubes that branch from the trachea into the lungs. For example: one to each lung.

alveoli

Tiny air sacs where gas exchange happens. For example: about 480 million.

diaphragm

A sheet of muscle under the lungs. For example: contracts and flattens to breathe in.

intercostal muscles

Muscles between the ribs. For example: lift the ribs up and out.

respiration

The reaction in every cell that releases energy from glucose. For example: glucose + oxygen -> carbon dioxide + water.
