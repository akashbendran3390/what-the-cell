Building with carbon

Carbon can form four covalent bonds, so it builds chains, branches and rings: the backbones of all the molecules of life. In this lab you join sugars into polymers and break them apart again, compare starch, glycogen and cellulose, build fats and oils, and see how sugars on the cell surface let cells recognise each other.
