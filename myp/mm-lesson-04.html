Notes: Metallic bonding

metallic bond

Attraction between positive metal ions and a sea of shared electrons. For example: in copper.

delocalised electrons

Outer electrons free to move through the metal. For example: the sea of electrons.

malleable

Can be hammered into shape without breaking. For example: aluminium foil.

ductile

Can be pulled into wires. For example: copper wire.

conductor

Lets electricity or heat pass through. For example: copper in cables.

lustre

Shiny surface. For example: gold, polished steel.
