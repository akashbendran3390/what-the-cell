Practical

Temperature and membrane permeability

Lab50 minB2.1.11, B2.1.12

You will need: Beetroot cylinders, water baths from 20 to 70 C, test tubes, colorimeter or colour chart

  1. Rinse the beetroot until the water runs clear
  2. Leave equal pieces in water at each temperature for 20 minutes
  3. Compare the colour of the water
  4. Plot the results, and explain them using membrane fluidity

Safety: Take care with hot water and with cutting tools. Beetroot stains.

Carry this out with your teacher's supervision and follow your school's lab rules.
