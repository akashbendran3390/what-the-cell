Notes: The nervous system and neurones

central nervous system (CNS)

The brain and spinal cord. For example: the coordinator.

peripheral nervous system

All the nerves outside the CNS. For example: nerves in your arms and legs.

neurone

A nerve cell that carries electrical impulses. For example: up to 1 m long.

axon

The long fibre that carries the impulse away from the cell body. For example: covered in a fatty sheath.

dendrites

Short branches that receive impulses from other neurones. For example: many on each cell.

nerve

A bundle of many axons. For example: the sciatic nerve in the leg.
