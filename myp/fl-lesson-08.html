Lesson 8: Heavier than air

How does a 70-tonne aircraft get off the ground? In this lesson you meet lift, weight, thrust and drag and try four Bernoulli stations.

By the end of the lesson you can

  • Name and describe the four forces on an aircraft
  • Compare the forces at take-off, cruising and landing
  • State Bernoulli's principle and use it to explain demonstrations
  • Explain how the shape of an aerofoil helps make lift

Notes: Heavier than air

aerofoil

A wing shape: rounded front, curved top, thin back.

drag

The force of air resisting motion; doubling the speed makes it about four times bigger. For example: why cars use more fuel at high speed.

Key words

liftupward force from air over a wing
thrustforward force from engines or propellers
dragair resistance
weightpull of gravity
aerofoilwing shape with a curved top
Bernoulli's principlefaster fluid, lower pressure
streamlinedsmooth shape with low drag
