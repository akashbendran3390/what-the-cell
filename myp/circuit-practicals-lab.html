Circuit Practicals Lab

Four practicals from the unit, done online: set the equipment, take readings, and watch your table and graph fill in. Use the same questions as the practical booklet.

Practical 4: current and p.d.

Component
Voltmeter0.00 V
Ammeter0.000 A
ComponentV / VI / AR / Ω
Take readings from 1 V to 6 V for the resistor, then for the lamp. Both appear on one graph.

Questions

  1. Describe the shape of each graph.
  2. Which component obeys Ohm’s law? Use your R values as evidence.
  3. Explain why the lamp’s resistance changes.

Practical 5: resistance of a wire

Wire
Voltmeter0.00 V
Ammeter0.00 A
WireL / cmV / VI / AR / Ω
Supply: 2.0 V. Record lengths from 10 cm to 100 cm, then try a different wire.

Questions

  1. Describe the relationship between length and resistance.
  2. Compare the thin and thick constantan wires. What does this show?
  3. Why do short lengths make the wire hot?

Practical 6: fuse test

FuseMelted at / AShort circuit?
Turn up the power pack slowly and watch the fuse wire. In real life only your teacher does this, behind a screen.

Questions

  1. Explain why the fuse wire melts and how this protects the circuit.
  2. Why does a higher-rated (thicker) fuse need a larger current to melt?
  3. A 220 V hair dryer is rated 1500 W. Calculate its current and choose a fuse from 3 A, 5 A, 10 A and 13 A.
